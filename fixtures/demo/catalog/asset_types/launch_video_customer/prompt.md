# Launch Video: customer

A customer-facing narrated video summarising what this launch brings. Title scene; a short overview
of the launch theme; one scene per solution, covering the customer problem it solves, what it does
and who benefits; and a closing scene with a call to talk to the account team. Cover only solutions
whose GTM gate approval is "approved" AND whose lifecycle stage is Beta, Pilot, GA or later, and say
clearly when something is in Beta or Pilot. Leave out everything else (opportunity or definition
stage, gate pending, stage not set) without mentioning it, and list what was left out in
open_questions for the approver. Benefit-led and confident, with no roadmap promises, no dates
beyond those in the context snapshot, no pricing unless it is stated, and no internal names, owners
or limitations wording meant for staff.
