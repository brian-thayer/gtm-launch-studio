# Brand Voice and Style Guide (demo placeholder)

> Injected into every writer and judge prompt. Keep it concise: the judges score against it.

## Voice
- **Confident, not arrogant.** State what the product does plainly. Avoid superlatives we cannot prove ("best", "only", "#1") unless they appear in the approved claims.
- **Customer-first.** Lead with the customer's problem and outcome, then the capability.
- **Clear over clever.** Short sentences. Active voice. No jargon without explanation.
- **Human.** Write the way a knowledgeable colleague would explain it.

## Style rules
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Spell out acronyms on first use (e.g. "Contact Centre as a Service (CCaaS)").
- Use British/International English spelling consistently within an asset (e.g. "centre", "optimise").
- Numbers: numerals for 10 and above; always numerals for metrics and percentages.
- Never promise future capabilities, dates or roadmap items in external assets.
- Beta and Pilot capabilities must be labelled as such in every asset that mentions them.
- Internal-only assets must carry the label "INTERNAL ONLY - DO NOT SHARE".

## Tone by audience
| Audience | Tone |
|---|---|
| Sales / internal | Direct, practical, punchy. Bullets welcome. |
| Customers / external | Warm, benefit-led, credible. Proof over adjectives. |
| Executives | Concise, outcome and metric oriented. |
