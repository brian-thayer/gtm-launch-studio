# Legal and Disclosure Language (demo placeholder)

This is invented text for the demo. It is not legal advice and not any real company's policy.

- **Beta:** every external mention says the capability is in Beta, may change and is not covered by the standard service commitments.
- **Pilot:** every external mention says the capability is in a limited pilot and available by invitation only.
- **General availability:** say a capability is generally available only when the context snapshot says so.
- **Forward-looking statements:** external assets contain no commitments about future features, dates or pricing. Where a date is unavoidable, add: "Plans are subject to change and are not a commitment to deliver."
- **Comparisons:** a comparison with a named competitor appears only in internal assets.
