# Fabrikam Engage — competitive profile (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

## Positioning
Fabrikam Engage is a marketing-led customer engagement platform with journey orchestration,
positioning on personalised omnichannel campaigns.

## Strengths
- Strong journey builder and marketing segmentation.
- Rich digital channels including email, push and WhatsApp.

## Weaknesses
- No native contact centre: handoff to live agents depends on third-party integrations, and agents
  often lack conversation context.
- Voice conversations limited; AI voice agents not native.

## How to win against Fabrikam Engage
- Lead with the closed loop: outreach, AI conversation and live-agent handoff in one Vonage platform.
- Bring the contact centre operations leader into the deal alongside marketing.

## Landmines
- "What happens when a customer replies to a campaign and needs to talk to someone?"
