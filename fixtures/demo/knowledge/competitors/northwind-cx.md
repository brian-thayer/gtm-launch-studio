# Northwind CX — competitive profile (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

## Positioning
Northwind CX positions itself as an all-in-one cloud contact centre suite for large enterprises,
leading with workforce engagement and analytics.

## Strengths
- Broad suite with mature workforce management and quality management modules.
- Strong brand recognition with large enterprise contact centres.

## Weaknesses
- Outbound engagement relies on a separate campaign module with list-based dialling; event-triggered
  outreach requires professional services and custom integration work.
- AI virtual agents are sold as a separate add-on with its own console.
- Long implementation timelines reported by prospects (multi-month deployments).

## How to win against Northwind CX
- Lead with time-to-value: no-code CRM event triggers versus custom integration projects.
- Show the unified agent desktop handoff with conversation summary.
- Ask about the total cost of the separate AI add-on and professional services.

## Landmines
- "How long did it take to launch your last outbound campaign, end to end?"
- "Which teams have to be involved to change a trigger rule today?"
