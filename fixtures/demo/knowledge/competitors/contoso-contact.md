# Contoso Contact — competitive profile (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

## Positioning
Contoso Contact is a dialler-first outbound platform popular with collections and sales teams,
positioning on agent productivity and dialling speed.

## Strengths
- Fast predictive and progressive dialling with strong list management.
- Competitive per-seat pricing for outbound-heavy teams.

## Weaknesses
- Primarily agent-led calling; limited AI-driven self-service conversations.
- Consent and calling-window rules configured per campaign rather than centrally.
- Limited digital channels (SMS only, no WhatsApp).

## How to win against Contoso Contact
- Reframe from "more dials per agent" to "fewer calls needed": AI resolves routine intents without an agent.
- Highlight centrally enforced consent and calling windows for compliance teams.

## Landmines
- "What share of your outbound calls could be resolved without an agent?"
- "How do you prove consent was checked before each contact?"
