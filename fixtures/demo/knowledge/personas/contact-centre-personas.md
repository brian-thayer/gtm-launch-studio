# Contact centre buyer personas (ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE)

## Contact Centre Operations Leader
- Measured on cost-to-serve, service level, first-contact resolution and agent attrition.
- Buys outcomes: fewer avoidable contacts, better-prepared agents.

## Campaign / Marketing Manager
- Measured on campaign conversion and speed to launch.
- Frustrated by dependence on IT for every campaign change.

## IT / Systems Architect
- Measured on security, integration effort and data governance.
- Needs data-flow diagrams, consent enforcement details and CRM connector documentation.
